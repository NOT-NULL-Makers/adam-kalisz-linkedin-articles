For nearly two decades, a programming language quietly changed how some of the world's best engineers think about software.

On April 16th, we're releasing *Clojure: The Official Documentary* — the story of Rich Hickey's obsessive, years-long quest to build something he couldn't find anywhere else: a language grounded in simplicity, immutability, and the kind of deep thinking most of the industry had stopped doing.

What started as one man's sabbatical project became a movement that reached the floors of Nubank, the corridors of Wall Street, and the laptops of engineers who quietly swear by it to this day.
