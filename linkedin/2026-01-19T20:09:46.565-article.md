We make a lot of fuss about insulation, yet a bit of isolation and air is enough to safeguard us from harm. Keeping our distance is a valid and effective course of action in some interpersonal relationships and quite technical fields as well.


Just like an electric circuit we need to protect ourselves from discharges, ground loops and interference. Even the best insulator will eventually break down and conduct the discharge somewhere damaging the very things it was supposed to protect. Therefore recognizing and clearly communicating limits early is essential.


Relying on the assumption of common perception of a situation when in reality the viewpoints couldn't be any different again leads to currents in undesired places.


Too many strong signals will eventually disturb machines and people alike. There is no shame in tuning out the noise and seemingly missing out on something to find the few signals we wholeheartedly cherish.


When everything is within reach we don't have to surmount obstacles, think it over many times and look forward to achieve the goal. In some ways, expertise is as much about striving and focus as it is about avoiding distractions.
