🟢🔵 Clojure/ Conj 2025 is over and everybody is on their way home, digesting all the new ideas and viewpoints.


Attending conferences is important because you can clarify things much more quickly when everybody is on the same page and focused on exchanging ideas about certain topics.

Also, you have the opportunity to learn about stuff you would never even think about. Last but not least you get to meet interesting people and can find new friendships, jobs, partners, customers, suppliers, supporters and more.


A conference is like a marketplace for knowledge workers in the sense that ideally everybody should leave with better value than what they arrived with.


I have learned a lot about designing scalable and reliable distributed systems, building communities and new approaches to solving some challenges. I have also learned about the struggles and successes of people from around the world that happen to use some of the same technologies as we do.
