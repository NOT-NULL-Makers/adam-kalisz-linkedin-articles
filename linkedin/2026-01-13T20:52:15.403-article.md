Many people would never do business with their own family. However I am convinced that founding a business with my brother Vít Kalisz was one of the best decisions.


A successful family business among siblings is not some historic anomaly. The brothers Siemens, Baťa, The Disney Brothers, Werner Bros., Michelin, even the somewhat infamous Lehman Brothers. A more recent example is Patrick and John Collison, who created Stripe.


A family business has unique risks but also advantages. If the company is doing great, usually the family also does. But differences in the business can have negative effect on the relationships in the family. Disagreements can sometimes be resolved for instance by splitting the business, like the Dassler brothers with Adidas and Puma or the Albrecht brothers with Aldi Nord/ Süd and Trader Joe’s. Sometimes the business can be sold or one of the parties can be bought out. Other times things don't go well unfortunately.


The most important thing with multiple founders is to have and recognize that everybody has complementary skills and some overlaps also. That differences within boundaries create better overall results and prevent whole classes of potential problems.

There needs to be deep trust and respect for each other. It's important to speak about values, share experiences and insights even if it might be embarrassing short-term. Sometimes it's necessary to support each other or to take on more responsibilities for some time when the other cannot.


It seems to me and I hope, some of the trust and respect for each other also trickles into our partnerships and customer relationships. Such relationships make our work deeply satisfying.
