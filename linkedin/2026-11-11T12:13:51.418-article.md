We are pleased to announce that EuroClojure 2027 will be held under the auspices of the City of Prague and Daniel Mazur, Prague City Councillor for ICT, Smart City, Science, Research, and Innovations.


EuroClojure 2027 will be the seventh edition of the international technology conference, taking place in Prague from 19 to 21 May 2027. One day of workshops at Břevnov Monastery, two days of talks in OREA Hotel Pyramida, a business evening at Červený Jelen, and a final social event in the monastery's historic cellars will provide plenty of opportunities for thoughtful conversations, technical exchange, and valuable business networking.


For us at NOT NULL Makers, this is more than a formal endorsement. It is a statement that Prague is a technology hub and a place where a conference like EuroClojure is welcome.


We are thankful for this vote of confidence and to everyone already helping to make EuroClojure 2027 worthy of it.
