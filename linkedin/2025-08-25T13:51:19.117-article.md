Random virtual machine freezes after migration to #Proxmox 9.


With a customer, we migrated a virtualization cluster from Proxmox 8.1.2 to the current Proxmox 9 during a maintenance window. All went well until we were basically done when one of the VMs we have migrated froze and was completely unresponsive.


This is a normal AMD64/ x86-64 virtual machine with #Debian 12 inside it. The CPU type was set to kvm64 otherwise nothing special with it. It serves as a legacy #OpenVPN concentrator and has also some firewall/ NAT duties more or less related to this function. This VM ran for 2 years without any issues before that. The underlying hardware is a Dell PowerEdge R620 server.


There is no obvious clue what might cause these freezes. There are no illuminating logs on the host or in the guest besides the usual churn. None of the other VMs misbehave but they are more or less idle most of the time.


The plan is to create a fresh VM with Debian 13 and hope that this will be a sufficient workaround for now before we understand, why this is happening.
