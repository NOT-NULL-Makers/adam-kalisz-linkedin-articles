How to get 300 professionals and I would say in many cases industry veterans into a single room for a day? Well, JUXT seem to have the recipe, because #XT26 was an absolute blast.


The common notion was that AI does not significantly speed up building understanding as Peter Naur described it, which is what we want in some, but not necessarily all, places. That's why we can have modules in our systems that get built in some “dark factory”, those are the less interesting parts, parts which also might be a library written by some person on the internet but that seems trustworthy or good enough to include into our project.


We might guard the quality and also help make the AI assisted development more efficient by better testing, e.g. using specs or property-based, generative testing. In #Clojure we do have #spec for that, in #Python there is #hypothesis.


XT26 was a day packed with sharing of experience, deep thoughts and conversations with great people.
