🧐 Does your vibe coding tool support #IPv6?


👉 I have looked at popular tools and services in the AI/ LLM space and came back with some insights.


🤦 For one, #ChatGPT does support IPv6, OpenAI does not.

🔃 There are obvious typo-squatting names like Grok (X-ai) and Groq, which is unrelated to Z-ai, which also exists. The more reputable ones do support IPv6.

🇨🇳 Chinese providers like DeepSeek and Qwen (Alibaba) don't really support IPv6 even though there is a strong government pressure to support and migrate to IPv6.

🛠️ Many vibe coding platforms and services building on LLMs tend to only support the legacy IPv4 protocol, Lovable almost seems like an exception to the rule.


👉 In summary support for IPv6 isn't bad if you look at the infrastructure, the providers actually developing the models with the exception of the Chinese ones the major providers generally do support IPv6.


👉 If you start look at the services that build on top of this infrastructure the support starts to be spotty.


👉 Looking at the domains and the IPv6 support does mirror the speed of development in the space and the focus on detail or the lack thereof.
