It seems like you can meet with many people from the open source world in Prague this week. On Tuesday, Vít Kalisz and I have used the opportunity and the nice weather to meet Wendy Randolph and Ingy döt Net known in the #Clojure circles. During the walk through the city we discussed many topics and were able to join the Clojure dev call from the hotel lobby in the evening. We hope to make #EuroClojure just as welcoming!
