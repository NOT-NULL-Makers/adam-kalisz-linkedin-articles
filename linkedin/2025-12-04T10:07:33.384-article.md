🪙 Did we decrease the cost of software development with the use of LLMs?


📈 Software development, especially of big regulated systems, tends to be way more expensive than initially imagined. Already in 1968 the NATO Software Engineering Conference at Garmisch, Germany recognized that there is a problem or a “software crisis”.


🟢🔵 Attending #Clojure/ Conj 2025 showed me that there are people giving this problem a serious thought. Starting from the language Clojure, continuing with databases and processing systems such as Datomic and Rama to small dialects such as Squint, libraries, tools and mini-frameworks like Component.


👉 And of course the problem is as much about technology as it is about organization and communication.
