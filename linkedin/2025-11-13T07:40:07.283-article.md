Focus, power, simplicity. I was able to validate these statements about #Clojure today in the Rama and Datomic workshops.


Nathan Marz of Red Planet Labs showed me today how Dataflow, Specter and their amalgamation in Rama can really make writing distributed systems better in so many ways if you are ready to put in the mental effort. It is a very strong contender if you need extreme levels of scalability with relatively little extra effort up front.


The #Datomic team also did a splendid job of explaining how to work with the query language Datalog and how the temporal awareness works in the database. I especially liked the explanations about the various indexes and how they allow Datomic to behave like a row or document, column, key-value, and graph database depending what order you index on entities, attributes, values and transactions.


Of course meeting everybody later on was a treat and allowed for some highly interesting conversations and some fun as well. Rich Hickey started working on Clojure 20 years ago and several people that I met today work with Clojure for more than 15 years. It's great when you can fit many of the people that made the language and ecosystem into the workhorse it is today and allowed the small but vibrant community to thrive around it into one admittedly big room.
