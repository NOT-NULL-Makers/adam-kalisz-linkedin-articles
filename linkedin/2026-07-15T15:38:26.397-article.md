EuroClojure 2027 has just received the official patronage of the Czech Ministry of Industry and Trade. 🎉

The Ministry has granted its patronage to the upcoming seventh edition of the international technology conference, taking place in Prague from 19–21 May 2027. 

For us at NOT NULL Makers, this is more than a formal endorsement. It is welcome recognition that bringing EuroClojure back after a decade is an undertaking worth supporting.

We are working to create three thoughtful days of workshops, talks, technical exchange, and conversations between people who care about building software and systems that last. 💡

Thank you to the Ministry and to Karel Havlíček personally for their confidence, and to everyone already helping us make EuroClojure 2027 worthy of it.

See you in Prague.
