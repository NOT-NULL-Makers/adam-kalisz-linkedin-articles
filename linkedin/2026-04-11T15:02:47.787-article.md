What should've been a simple article about Albanian spying history turned out to be a lot more revealing than I expected.


First, I wanted to transcribe the table in the picture using Google Gemini 3.1 Pro within the paid subscription. It did so, but while I was actually checking the output I uncovered about 20 instances, where the Optical Character Recognition just didn't do a great job for no obvious reason. There were about two mistakes that I would attribute not to bad OCR, but some kind of rewrite, such as switching digits though.


To make sure I didn't overlook something myself, I did the totals for each year and wanted to check that against the total written in the table. This is where I uncovered that the numbers for 4 years don't add up. The years 1960 and 1984 are probably a digit mixup, the years 1958 and 1963 are just wrong as I see it. This is a little embarrassing considering it's the statistics of collaborators who spied on their fellow citizens between the years 1949 and 1990. Do they also count the victims with this kind of diligence?


This also shows how difficult fact-checking is. How many thousands of people walked by without anybody noticing or reporting the inaccuracy?
