Do you know who can have a mediocre Lighthouse score?


Google can. Their authentication dialog is painfully slow and everybody just accepts it. Would you guess, who else can have a slowish website and it will not change your behavior?


That's right, banks can. If you want to do something with your money or your debt, you will just wait that extra few seconds. Government websites that I have tested aren't worse, I was somewhat surprised. You do have to pay taxes after all.


So web performance is relative. You don't want to be too bad at it but if you are in a position of power, you don't have to chase the all-green Lighthouse mobile score. It's just embarrassing, when a login dialog with a single button gets a worse score than a full website of the European union even though it belongs to the same company that did the benchmark and the tool that runs it.
