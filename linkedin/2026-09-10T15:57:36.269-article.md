We are pleased to announce that EuroClojure 2027 will be held under the auspices of Zdeněk Zajíček, President of the Hospodářská komora České republiky (Czech Chamber of Commerce).


EuroClojure 2027 will be the seventh edition of the international technology conference, taking place in Prague from 19–21 May 2027. 


For us at NOT NULL Makers, this is more than a formal endorsement. It is a welcome recognition that bringing EuroClojure back after a decade and discussing how to build and operate reliable information systems is an undertaking worth supporting.


Thank you to Zdeněk Zajíček personally and his team for their confidence, and to everyone already helping us make EuroClojure 2027 worthy of it.


We are looking forward to hosting three thoughtful days of workshops, talks, technical exchange, conversations, and valuable business networking in Prague.
