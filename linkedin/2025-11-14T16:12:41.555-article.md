The great thing about small-ish communities is that you can get a big part of the community into a single place at once to meet and share ideas.


In his talk, Rich Hickey offered the view point that a conference is about giving in the sense that sharing ideas should feel like giving/ receiving to the involved parties. Especially in the age of LLMs we have to decide if we want to spend our time in conversations with robots or with people.
