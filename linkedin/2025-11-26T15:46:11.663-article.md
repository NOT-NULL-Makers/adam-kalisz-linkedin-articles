⚙️ The #Proxmox Backup Server 4.1 release has the potential for large speedups especially when constrained by the chunk loading and verification.


👉 This basically dates back to the Proof of Concept Dominik Csapak did after our common effort this summer to speed up backup restores. If you use NVMe as the backing store in some cases a 2x speedup could be possible according to Christian Ebner of Proxmox. Larger speedups are reported by the community. So thank you Nicolas Frey for pushing this over the finish line!


🦾 With advancements like these the case for proprietary backup solutions grows thinner with each release it seems!
