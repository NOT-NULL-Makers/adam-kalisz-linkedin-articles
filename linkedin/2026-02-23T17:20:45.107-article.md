Last week, I analyzed 86 high-impact Czech DNS domains and their TXT records especially for OpenAI and Anthropic domain verification codes. Today I looked at 5158 Czech domains filtered out of Cloudflare 1M top domain dataset from last week.


It turns out that 59 of them have OpenAI ChatGPT and 10 have Anthropic Claude verification. Only Air Bank a.s. and Conseq Investment Management, a.s. have both.


As far as I can tell, only College of Polytechnics, Jihlava does have OpenAI verification, I didn't find others. The Scio network of schools is also prominent enough to reach the top 1 million domains, and they have OpenAI verification as well.


I was surprised to find the municipalities of Vyškov, Mikulov and Albrechtice seem to use OpenAI.


Of course, I have also found a number of “interesting” domains this way, one obviously providing hourly hotels “secretly” (natajno). I wonder why they have an OpenAI verification?


Also I would like to thank Heureka.cz / sk for their professional approach in the comment section of the last article. After noticing they still had a legacy entry in the list of TXT records, they promptly cleaned it up. I like this approach and frank communication style. 👍
