Even advanced users of AI do two major mistakes that are somewhat related.



Firstly, people don't use Claude Code, Codex CLI, Antigravity, Opencode and similar agentic harnesses and if they do, it's often not segmented off into a virtual machine or a container but rather has access to most things on their computer protected mostly by wishful thinking.



Secondly, people don't segment their agentic work into VMs and containers because they don't know or don't feel comfortable using Secure SHell (SSH), tmux, sshfs or other network filesystems etc.



The advantage of using VMs or containers for harnesses on servers is that you don't have to walk around with a running laptop, don't have to sleep next to a humming computer, worry about the stability of your internet connection, the power supply or battery status too much and other inconveniences and “workarounds” like that.



Also, it is much harder for the agent to leak or delete data that were out of scope for the work. In many cases, there are also better options how to snapshot or otherwise backup VMs or containers than doing granular backups on your laptop while you try to work.



Last but not least, your agents can connect to other systems over SSH and fetch data or do other useful work for you. This can even be setup in a way to be passwordless yet sufficiently restricted.
