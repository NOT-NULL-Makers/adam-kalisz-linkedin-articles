“Can I try to log in into the banking app again? I don't know if I used my birth certificate number or my personal ID number in the password.” is a sentence I overheard today while waiting in a bank.


An elderly man somehow uninstalled his banking app or couldn't login. After identifying himself with his national ID card, he received a one time access and was able to sign in. Obviously he was prompted to set up a new #password, which he did on the spot.


If you ever tried to be creative while sitting at a counter in the bank in a chair like shown in the photo, you can be sure you will have no good ideas. Which is precisely what happened here.


Please, don't use any ID numbers, dates of birth, names of your relatives, nicknames etc. Have a password manager or at least a crazy sentence without meaning that you can type in. Most smartphone applications will happily use the biometric identification so you will almost never need the password anyway.


To all the UX people at big institutions, don't make people think creatively under stress and in a completely boring environment of a branch office. You and your security colleagues can think of a way how to help people avoid shooting themselves in the foot from a security point of view and make the customer experience better.
