🤔 Why would you have a Domain Specific Language for the traditional Structured Query Language?


👉 Because of better composition, less object-relational impedance mismatch and code and queries in one place.


🦾 Also you get really used to structural editing with #Clojure and don't want to miss it when writing basically #SQL produced by the #HoneySQL library by Sean Corfield.
