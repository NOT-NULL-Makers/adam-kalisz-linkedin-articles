Last Friday, we've sent out the first #EuroClojure2027 newsletter. We plan EuroClojure for May 19–21, 2027 in Prague, Czechia. If you don't know the programming language #Clojure, fear not as the event will be friendly to everybody interested in building, evolving and operating reliable, well-performing systems.



We can confirm the venues, OREA Hotel Pyramida Praha for the talks on Thursday and Friday, and the stately halls of Břevnov Monastery for the workshops on Wednesday and the cellars for the social event on Friday evening. We will also have Hotel Adalbert on Břevnov grounds completely for us.



In the upcoming days and weeks we will reach out to sponsors, speakers, ensure we also have a venue for the business evening that is planned for Thursday and much more.
