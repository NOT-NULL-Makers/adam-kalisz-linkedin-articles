Did you ever wonder how QR codes actually work? How does practical spec-driven development work? QRity and clj-png-adapter answer that for me and the result is useful software for #Clojure and #ClojureScript also.


I took the specification, skimmed through it, from that sketched out the progressive implementation path. Started with simple data structures and defined the Minimal Viable Product in the form of a small version 1 QR code encode. From that went most of the way, always verifying against the spec and other implementations. When a reasonable subset was done, I took on the decode, which has to also work with real world and therefore less than ideal images. Again, the tests generate QR codes as images, mangle and otherwise transform them in random ways trying to read them.


Then I refocused on performance and memory usage. Based on profiling and educated guesses, switched to byte arrays. From Proof of Concept quality came something that has competitive performance compared with widely used libraries.


Then came the interop test. I want to run my libraries on #Babashka and #nbb also but they lack javax-imageio and Node does lack the canvas. But they have deflate/ zlib libraries, which enables us to write a small decode/ encode library that can consume a large subset of the PNG image format. It turns out, with a DecompressionStream/ CompressionStream we can also support canvas-less operation in the browser and sidestep the challenges with canvas. That way, you can avoid silent canvas deallocation on low memory or generate QR codes even with anti-tracking protections for example in the Tor browser.


Yes, both libraries were mostly developed by using Anthropics Claude Code and OpenAIs Codex with Fable 5, Opus 5 and GPT 5.6 Sol doing most of the work with at most medium level effort. The point here wasn't to implement Galois field computation and similar things by hand but to understand the various stages and aspects of not only QR code decode and encode but also this development style, when a detailed specification is available.
