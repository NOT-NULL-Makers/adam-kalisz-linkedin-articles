In the #Clojure programming language community Rich Hickey coined the term Hammock-driven development.


The talk from Clojure/Conj 2010 is about deep thinking. Yes, the one actually performed by humans and not the marketing term popularized by the various LLM offerings.


Now can you actually think deeply about a topic for an extended amount of time, perhaps over days or even weeks, without constantly re-reading information? Can you keep the relevant parts in your head?


One approach I practiced historically was to use the white back side of leftover A1 or A0 posters with a thin permanent marker because I will write and read it up close. Also, I can fold that sheet of glossy paper into the folder and bring it with me. Similarly, current LLMs need to summarize stuff to work with problems that exceed the content window.


What is your approach to cracking hard problems?
