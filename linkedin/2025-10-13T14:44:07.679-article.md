🤩 Going to Clojure/ Conj 2025 this November!


👉 On 13th of November, Rich Hickey, the creator of the programming language #Clojure will kick things off with a short welcome. After that there will be many talks I look forward to, for instance:


• Paula Gearon will talk about how Clojure can interact with the #Python ecosystem through libpython-clj by Chris Nuernberger.


• David Nolen will show us how to survive on the world wide web with ClojureScript.


• Christophe Grand and Baptiste DUPUCH will share progress in the ClojureDart and Flutter land.


• And it seems I will learn about Clockwork from Paolo Ferri. Clockwork is a new protocol-driven Clojure runtime hosted in Swift.


• Jeaye Wilkerson ports Clojure faithfully to LLVM to enable seamless interoperability with C++ and open the door for Clojure in the tripple A game industry. Jank is an ambitious project but the humble name already suggests Jeaye is fully aware of the scope.


• Michiel Borkent is known for several tools many in the community use a lot. Babashka, clj-kondo, nbb, sci, Scittle among others. Especially if you don't know about Clojure and just want to get started quickly I suggest you look into babashka. It can replace a lot of your Bash and Python scripts easily.


I am also looking forward to the workshops and chats between the talks. It will be an opportunity to meet people face to face and definitely worth the 7500 km of travel from Prague, Czechia to Charlotte, North Carolina. I should arrive on 11th of November already so please let me know if you want to grab a bite together.
